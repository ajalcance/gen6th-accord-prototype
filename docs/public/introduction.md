# Gen6th Accord

Gen6th Accord is accountability infrastructure for work made by people, institutions and AI
agents. It records who asserted what, about which version of a work, with what evidence, and who
approved it, and it lets anyone check that record later without having to trust Accord.

## What Accord does

- **Collab:** governed collaboration with roles, reviews, contributions and releases.
- **Claims:** structured assertions about authorship, contribution, rights and more, with their
  evidence, confirmations, disputes and appeals.
- **Trust:** signed records, a public transparency log and independent verification.

## What Accord does not do

Accord does not decide legal ownership, certify that a claim is true or replace a court,
regulator, publisher or ethics body. A valid signature proves who signed what and when. It does
not prove the claim is correct.
