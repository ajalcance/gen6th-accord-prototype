# ADR-0048: Synthetic-performer identity and reference-based search

**Date:** 2026-10-05 (Asia/Manila)
**Status:** Accepted product/design direction by the owner; implementation and activation pending.
**Deciders:** Repository owner

## Context

The owner frames AI Persona Passport as infrastructure for an emerging synthetic-performer
industry: recognizable AI actors across dramas, films, advertisements and other productions.
Studios need continuing asset identity, creation/contribution evidence, authorized uses and
investigation of suspicious appearances. A portrait registration ID alone cannot supply search.
The owner approved the studio features and recognition recommendations, then clarified that
present legal acceptance must not limit this forward-looking product objective.

## Decision

1. Pursue persistent, searchable synthetic-performer identity through approved reference
   enrollment, face analysis, model-dependent representations and immutable version history.
   Distinguish the performer from a role/character and a particular generated image.
2. Extend the hackathon journey with measured catalogue search and production-use investigation/
   clearance. Support attributed rights claims and evidence, independent review, specific
   authorizations and eventual platform-signed passport integrity. First-mover status is an
   ambition to substantiate, not a factual claim established by this decision.
3. Preserve explicit Scan and the existing inspected-upload requirement. Add face selection/
   quality assessment and dedicated bounded analysis derivatives before representations.
   Existing 320-pixel thumbnails are not validated recognition inputs. Use exact/perceptual
   fingerprints plus evaluated face-centered/whole-image embeddings with complete provenance.
4. Permit search without new registration. Enforce studio-private, explicitly shared and
   opt-in catalogue boundaries; protect derived data and authorize the index/query path.
   Group candidates by performer; expose matched references, coverage, uncertainty and failures.
5. Select concrete models, quality rules and ranking through permitted synthetic-performer
   evaluation. Test copies, regenerated appearances and unrelated lookalikes separately;
   no calibrated identity probability, universal uniqueness or ownership verdict is implied.
   Gemini evidence assistance and human interpretation remain distinct from visual retrieval.
6. Keep additional canonical references owner-approved; no automatic enrollment of discoveries.
   Retain competing claims and distinguish receipt time, declarations, reviewed evidence and
   external rights records. A passport can support future industry conventions without claiming
   that platform registration creates statutory IP rights or current government recognition.
7. Stage studio-supplied appearance checks before broader permitted web discovery, video/voice
   support and monitoring. Retain source context and human outcomes. Preserve the full roadmap
   while delivering a bounded working prototype in Media, Content & Digital Experiences.
8. Demonstrate the capture-to-serving technology and meaningful Google integration alongside
   the concept. [The Google reference architecture](../hackathon/google-capture-search-architecture.md)
   defines proposed service responsibilities, exact-search baseline, encrypted derived-data
   boundaries, durable jobs and technical evidence. Concrete integrations remain pending.

## Consequences

The product is designed for future industry infrastructure, rather than constrained to current
legal administration. Reliable recognition requires additional image processing, encrypted
derived records, authorized retrieval/indexing, lifecycle recovery and fresh measured evidence.
These are approved targets, not implemented schema/services. Concrete model/package selection,
installations, cloud spend, migrations/grants, providers, deployment and issuance activation
remain separately scoped. No guard, permission policy or runtime changes accompany this ADR.

Full requirements, approval provenance, delivery stages and acceptance are in
[the industry direction record](../hackathon/synthetic-performer-industry.md); current behavior
and activation limits remain in [as-built](../architecture/current-state.md).

## Alternatives considered

- ID-only registration cannot retrieve an unfamiliar appearance of a performer.
- Whole-image similarity without face evaluation may rank backgrounds or costumes.
- Automatically learning from search hits can contaminate canonical references.
- Global private-catalogue search leaks studio assets; shared discovery must be authorized.
- Waiting for a mature legal category postpones the approved industry-infrastructure experiment.
- Claiming ownership/uniqueness from enrollment or similarity exceeds the evidence.
