Third-party components remain subject to their own rights and licences. Do not claim
exclusive ownership of dependencies, template components or another creator's material.

the actual repository, its history, deck, demo and linked pages against that inventory.
Keep excluded material out of the submitted package; a heading cannot exclude content that
is actually supplied. Obtain written organizer acknowledgment of the intended boundary.
